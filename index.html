Guide

How to Use Simple POS

Simple POS is made for pop-ups, markets, small shops and events. You can use it to take orders, keep track of sales and show payment details to customers.

You do not need to create an account. Your products, orders and settings are saved on the device and browser you are using.

No account needed Easy to set up Saved on this device Light & dark mode
Start Here

Set up Simple POS

1
Open Customize
This is where you set up your business name, products, colors and payment details.
2
Add your business name and currency
Open General & Branding, enter the name you want to show at the top of the POS, and choose your currency.
3
Choose how it looks
You can add your logo, choose a ready-made theme, use your own brand colors, and switch between Light, Dark or System mode.
4
Add your products
Open Products & Categories and add products one by one, or import a product list from a CSV file.
5
Arrange your menu
Put categories in the order you want. You can also give special products their own color or a short note such as Today's Special.
6
Add payment details
Open Payment Information if you want to add a QR code or bank transfer details for customers.
7
Start taking orders
Go back to POS. You are ready to use it.
Already have a Simple POS setup file from someone else? Go to General & Branding → Configuration Backup & Transfer and import it. This can save you from setting everything up again.
Make It Yours

Change the look of your POS

Open Customize → General & Branding to make Simple POS match your business.

  • Change the business name at the top.
  • Add your logo.
  • Choose one of the ready-made themes.
  • Or choose Custom Palette to use your own two brand colors.
  • Choose Light, Dark or System mode.
Simple POS handles the supporting colors automatically so your chosen brand colors still work with readable backgrounds, text and controls.

Logo tip: wide or wordmark-style logos work best. A transparent PNG around 800 × 200 px is recommended.

The red Danger Zone is only for deleting or resetting data. You do not need to use it during normal setup.

Taking Orders

Take an order

  1. Choose a category, or tap All Products to see everything.
  2. Tap a product to add it to the order. Tap it again to add another one.
  3. If you added something by mistake, tap the − button next to it.
  4. Check the total and the order number.
  5. Choose how the customer paid: Cash, Card, Bank Transfer or QR Payment.
  6. Tap Place Order.
  7. Call out the order number when the order is ready.
  8. Open Sales and tap Complete once the order has been handed over.
Order numbers start again each day. If you mark an order as completed by mistake, you can reopen it.
Devices

Use it on a tablet, phone or computer

Simple POS is designed mainly for larger touch-screen devices such as iPads and tablets, but it also works on laptops, desktops and phones.

Landscape mode is recommended. The POS layout is optimized for wider screens, especially when taking orders quickly on a touch device.
  1. Open the Simple POS website in your browser.
  2. On an iPhone or iPad, Safari works well and lets you use Add to Home Screen for a more app-like experience.
  3. On phones, Simple POS is usable in portrait mode, but landscape mode is recommended for a more comfortable ordering layout.

On a laptop or desktop, you can also open the HTML file directly for testing or local use.

Products

Add and organize products

Go to Customize → Products & Categories to manage your menu or product list.

  • Add, edit or delete products whenever you need to.
  • Give each product a name, price and category.
  • Categories appear automatically when you add products to them.
  • All Products always stays at the top.
  • Use Category Order to move categories up or down.
  • If a category has no products left in it, it disappears automatically.
  • You can give a product its own color if you want it to stand out.
  • You can also add a short note such as Today's Special, New or Best Seller.
Add Many Products

Import a product list

If you have a lot of products, you can import them in bulk using a CSV file.

Your CSV can contain these columns:

Product name,Price,Category,Color,Note Americano,700,Coffee,, Iced Milo Dino,1000,Coffee,#FF5A2F,Today's Special T-Shirt,2500,Clothing,#2B6E4F,Featured
  • Product name, Price and Category are needed.
  • Color and Note are optional.
  • If you leave Color blank, the product will use your normal brand color.

You can download a template from Customize → Products & Categories. When importing, choose whether to add/update products while keeping the current list, or replace the product list completely.

Sales

Check your sales

Open Sales to see what you sold. It opens on today's sales first.

  • See how much you made.
  • See how many orders were placed.
  • See which orders are still open.
  • See how many items were sold.
  • See which products sold the most.
  • See how each customer paid.

To check an older day, choose a date from the calendar or use the previous and next day buttons.

To save a copy of the sales for the day you are looking at, tap Export Selected Day CSV.

Customer Payments

Show payment details

The Payment page is made to be shown to your customer.

In Customize → Payment Information, you can add:

  • A payment QR code
  • Bank name
  • Account name
  • Account number
  • Branch
  • Any extra payment instructions

When a customer needs the details, open Payment and turn the screen toward them.

Important

Where your information is saved

Simple POS saves your products, settings and sales on the device and browser you are using.

Closing or refreshing the page normally will not remove your information. However, clearing your browser's website data can remove it.

If you open Simple POS on another phone, tablet, computer or browser, your information will not automatically appear there.

It is a good idea to regularly export your sales, especially after an event or busy sales day.

Backup & Transfer

Move your setup to another device

If you want to use the same products and settings on another device, go to Customize → General & Branding → Configuration Backup & Transfer.

  • Tap Export Config to download a Simple POS setup file.
  • The file contains things such as your business name, products, categories, colors and payment details.
  • Your past sales are not included in this file.
  • You can choose to include your logo and payment QR code as well.
Importing a setup file will replace the products and settings currently on that device. Your existing sales history will stay there, but anything currently sitting in the order screen will be cleared.

If you only want to add or update products, use the CSV product import instead.

Good to Know

What Simple POS does not do yet

Simple POS is kept intentionally simple for pop-ups, markets and small events. It does not currently include:

  • Automatic syncing between devices
  • Online backups
  • Staff accounts or permissions
  • Stock tracking
  • Built-in card payments
  • Receipt printing
  • Automatic tax calculations
  • Refund tools
  • A customer list or database
  • A separate kitchen or preparation screen

Customize

General & Branding

Set the identity, currency and visual style used across the POS.

General

Set the business name and choose how prices are displayed.

Brand Logo

Upload a logo to replace the text business name in the header. Horizontal or wordmark logos work best. Aim for roughly a 4:1 aspect ratio (for example, 800 × 200 px). A transparent PNG is recommended; square or tall logos will appear smaller in the header.

Theme & Appearance

Start with a ready-made theme or choose Custom to use your own primary and secondary brand colors. Core backgrounds, cards, text and form controls use a protected light/dark neutral system so the interface stays readable.

System follows the device's light or dark appearance.
Custom palette: choose only your brand's Primary and Secondary / CTA colors. Simple POS automatically derives readable text, borders and surfaces around them.
Primary
#1F4B99
Secondary / CTA
#2F6BD2
Contrast protection is on. Text and supporting UI colors are adjusted automatically when a brand color would be difficult to read.

Configuration Backup & Transfer

Export the POS setup as a single JSON configuration file and import it on another device or browser. The configuration includes your business settings, selected theme, light/dark preference, brand palette, products, product accents and notes, category order, currency, and payment information. Sales history and the current cart are never included.

Images are embedded directly inside the JSON file, so enabling this will make the file larger.
Importing replaces the current setup and product catalogue, preserves existing sales history, and clears the current cart. If the imported file does not contain images, your existing logo and QR code are left unchanged.

Products & Categories

Add products, use product-specific accent colors, import products and control category order.

Add / Edit Product

Categories come directly from your products. A category disappears automatically when no products use it.

A short label shown on the product card using that product's accent color.

Category Order

Choose the order categories appear in the POS. All Products always stays at the top.

Products

Edit a product to change its name, price, category, accent color or menu note. Delete the final product in a category to remove that category.

CSV Import

Import Product name, Price and Category. Optional columns are Color (a hex value such as #FF5A2F) and Note (for example, Today's Special).

Payment Information

Configure the QR code and bank details shown on the customer-facing Payment page.

Payment Details

Upload a QR code image and enter the bank details you want customers to see.

⚠ Danger Zone

Destructive actions only. Changes made here can permanently remove locally stored POS data.

Reset Entire POS

This removes products, settings, branding, payment details, the current cart and sales history stored in this browser.

This data is stored locally. Resetting it cannot be undone unless you have exported or backed up the information elsewhere.
